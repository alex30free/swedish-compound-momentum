How Compound Momentum Works

01 · What It Measures

Compound Momentum sums a stock's raw percentage price return over three distinct time windows — 3 months, 6 months, and 12 months. A stock that rises strongly across all three windows achieves a high composite score. No weighting: each window contributes equally.

02 · Why Three Windows?

Different momentum horizons capture different phenomena. The 3-month window captures short-term trend acceleration. The 6-month window filters noise and confirms the trend. The 12-month window ensures structural, sustained outperformance — not a brief spike.

03 · Size Class

Market cap is read in its own currency from Yahoo Finance. Large is at least about SEK 11 bn, Small is below about SEK 1.65 bn, and Mid sits between, following Nasdaq Nordic's EUR 1 bn and EUR 150 m bands at an approximate 11 SEK/EUR. Small caps trade thinner and move more, so each class is ranked on its own.

05 · MA200 Trend Check

The 200-day moving average is the line between an intact uptrend and a broken one. A close above it means the trend is intact; a close below it means it has broken. The page shows the side, the distance in percent, and the date of the last crossing. Each run uses the latest daily closes.

06 · Strength Ranking

Strength converts each stock's rank into a 0–100 scale across all screened stocks: 100 is the strongest, 0 the weakest. It gives one clear order to choose from. The buy list then applies the trend and liquidity checks on top.

04 · Piotroski F-Score

A 0–9 quality score based on 9 binary signals across profitability (ROA, cash flow, margins), leverage (debt ratio, current ratio, dilution), and efficiency (operating margin, return on equity). Higher scores indicate stronger financial health.

Compound Momentum Score = Return(3M) + Return(6M) + Return(12M)
Simple Momentum Strategy · Rules
  1. Universe. Take the screener list and split it into Large, Mid and Small cap using the size bands above.
  2. Pick. In each class, take the top 5 by compound score that are above their MA200 and trade at least 2 MSEK a day on average over 60 days. These are the buy-list names.
  3. Size. Give the chosen names equal weight. How much you put in each class is your own decision.
  4. Review weekly. Check again after each Friday update. Consider selling when a holding closes below its MA200 or drops out of the top 10 of its class. This exit rule is a suggestion, not a tested rule.
  5. Respect the risk. Small caps can have wide spreads and sharp reversals. Momentum has long drawdowns, and a stock can fall fast after a strong run.

Background: Jegadeesh and Titman (1993) documented that US stocks that did well over 3 to 12 months tended to keep outperforming in the months after. Faber (2007) found that a simple 10-month moving average rule reduced drawdowns across several asset classes; the MA200 here is a daily version of that idea. Evidence from Nordic markets is thinner. This strategy has not been backtested on this page, so test it before you rely on it. Educational content only, not financial advice.
